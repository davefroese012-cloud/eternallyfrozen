About Chloe

After years of dedicated training under Chris Pecor at Dead Man Worx, Chloe recently completed her full apprenticeship. She specializes in hyper-realistic portraits, intricate dark gothic pieces, and Renaissance-inspired work. Currently tattooing at Dead Man Worx in Chilliwack while building her own brand, Eternally Frozen.

Her passion lies in creating timeless, emotionally resonant tattoos that feel both classical and contemporary.
