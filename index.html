About Chloe

After completing her apprenticeship under Chris Pecor at Dead Man Worx, Chloe launched Eternally Frozen. She specializes in hyper-realistic portraits, intricate dark gothic work, and Renaissance-inspired tattoos.

Her work blends classical technique with emotional depth, creating pieces that feel both timeless and deeply personal.
